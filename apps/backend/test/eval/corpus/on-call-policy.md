# On-call Policy

On-call rotations run weekly, Monday 09:00 to Monday 09:00. Each engineer is on call at most one week in four.

## Swaps and leave
To swap a shift, agree the swap with a teammate and record it in PagerDuty at least 48 hours before the shift starts.
Before taking leave that overlaps your shift, you must arrange a swap first; leave requests are not approved while
the shift is uncovered.

## Compensation
Each on-call week earns one day of time off in lieu, taken within the following two months.
